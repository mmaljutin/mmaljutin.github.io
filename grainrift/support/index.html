Grainrift support

Found a bug, lost progress or have an idea? Write to mmaljutin@gmail.com. Please mention your iPhone model and, if you can, attach a screenshot.

Common questions

Does the game work offline?
Yes. Ads need an internet connection, but everything else works without one.

Is my progress saved?
Yes, automatically and only on your device, including in the middle of a level.

I bought something and reinstalled the game.
Open Shop and tap Restore purchases.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?
Menu → Settings.

How do I change my ad privacy choice?
Menu → Settings → Privacy options.
