Grainrift privacy policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Grainrift is a single-player game. It has no accounts and no sign-in, and the developer does not collect or store any personal information about you. This page explains what happens with data while you play.

Your game progress

Your progress and settings are saved only on your device. They are not sent to the developer. If you delete the game, this data is deleted too.

Advertising

The game can show optional video ads, only when you choose to watch one for a bonus. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show ads and measure them, Google may collect information such as your IP address, device type, operating system, app version and how you interact with ads. The game does not ask for access to your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. Without consent, only non-personalized ads are shown.

How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID, only a confirmation that a purchase was made so that the game can unlock it.

Children

Grainrift is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Since the developer does not hold personal data about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. For data collected by Google for advertising, see Google's policy linked above. If you have any questions about privacy, write to mmaljutin@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
