Light Meter privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Light Meter is an exposure meter and shooting journal for film cameras. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer does not collect or store any personal information about you. Everything the app keeps stays on your iPhone.

Camera

The camera is used to measure how bright the scene is. The live image is processed on your iPhone and is not recorded or sent anywhere. A picture is kept only when you tap the button to log a frame: it is saved in the app's journal and, if you allow it, added to your Photos library. The app asks only for permission to add photos, not to see your library.

Location

If you allow it, the place where you log a frame is saved with that frame, so it shows on a map in the journal and in Photos. Location is used only at that moment and is stored only on your iPhone. You can turn it off in Settings → Save location with frames, or in the iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. Maps in the journal are shown by Apple Maps, which receives the map area needed to draw them under Apple's privacy policy.

Your journal

Rolls, frames, notes and settings are saved only on your iPhone. They are not sent to the developer. A roll leaves the device only when you export it as a CSV file and choose where to share it. Your iPhone's own backup (for example, iCloud Backup) may include the app's data, as with any app. If you delete the app, its data is deleted too.

Purchases

Light Meter Pro and tips are processed by Apple. The developer does not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID, only a confirmation that a purchase was made so that the app can unlock it.

Children

Light Meter is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

Since the developer does not hold personal data about you, there is nothing to access or delete on our side. If you have any questions about privacy, write to mmaljutin@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
